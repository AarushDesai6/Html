💪 Why your body needs Exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes everyday are happier, sleep better and do better at school.

Dancing, swimming, football, yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster counts. Keep moving and have fun!

☀ Quick Health Tips

  • Eat 5 fruits and veggies every day 🍎
  • Drink 8 glasses of water daily 🍻
  • Get enough sleep 😴
  • Wash your hands often 🙈
  • Play outside and get fresh air 🌱
