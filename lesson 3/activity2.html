Planet Earth - Quick Guide

Our planet is full of incredible records, species, and facts.Here is a quick guide using lists!

Steps to Protect the Planet

  1. Switch off the lights when leaving a room
  2. Use a reusable water bottle every day
  3. Walk or cycle instead of travelling by car
  4. Plant a tree in your neighborhood
  5. Spread awareness - talk to your friends and family

Incredible Natural Wonders:

  • The Great Barrier Reef - the largest coral reef, visible from space
  • The Amazon Rainforest - home to 10% of all species on Earth
  • Mount Everest - The highest point on Earth at 8,848 meters
  • Sahara Desert - the largest hot desert in the world

Key terms:

Climate Change
Long term shift in global temprature caused by human activity
